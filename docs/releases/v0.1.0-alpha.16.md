# sealr 0.1.0-alpha.16

Alpha.16 contains typed publisher outcomes, tested consumer migrations, named
Rust durability controls, and reproducible assurance history. It keeps the
existing twelve explicit format selections and their semantic identities.

This is a development preview for integration and adversarial testing, not a
stable API freeze, independent security audit, or production-containment claim.
This GitHub-only prerelease does not publish a crate to crates.io. The
[immutable release page](https://github.com/blisspixel/sealr/releases/tag/v0.1.0-alpha.16)
records publication state; [release verification](../release-verification.md)
defines checksum, provenance, and immutable-record checks.

## Machine-readable publisher decisions

The [Deepr content gate](../wheel-content-gate.md) now emits
`sealr.deepr-content-gate-failure.v1` with explicit stages, codes, and available
finding codes. Setup, acquisition, archive, evidence, wheel-semantic, and
publisher-policy failures can be handled without parsing diagnostic text.
The successful `sealr.deepr-content-gate.v1` report and exit-code contract remain
compatible. Failures never emit acceptance or claim installed files.

Evidence verification still precedes deletion of the private source copy.
Wheel evaluation and content checks then consume `VerifiedArchive`; the caller's
wheel is preserved. Linux process checks cover two accepted retention variants
and ten refusals, including a refusal after private-source removal.

The separate owner-maintained validation project replays this adapted consumer
against immutable Alpha.15 source and authenticated matching native artifacts.
Its bounded traces observe recognizable wheel pathnames and FD annotations in
the recorded process tree after deletion. They do not cover arbitrary aliases
or unseen processes. These historical reports remain Alpha.15 evidence, not
Alpha.16 artifact results or independent external adoption.

## Tested consumer migrations

The [candidate inventory](../candidate-surface.md) now binds all four planned
replacement rows to executable, versioned migration rules:

- Five packaged evidence cases accept the canonical lineage, refuse legacy and
  mixed pairs without conversion or fallback, and keep verified rejection
  evidence consumer-denied.
- The Deepr gate, PyPA handoff, and extracted-package consumer use actual
  `VerifiedArchive` authority and separate requested-effect checks. Six real
  outcome cases cover inspection, committed materialization, malformed
  structure, CRC denial, setup failure, and an unavailable source. A capability
  does not prove a requested write committed. The existing staged-content audit
  test separately proves that verified authority can survive late effect failure.
- `Durability::FlushOnly` and `Durability::MemberSync`, with methods on `Policy`
  and `PolicyDocument`, name existing completed-member behavior. The historical
  `atomic` field remains their sole serialized backing value. Tests preserve
  policy bytes, digests, validation, worker encoding, and actual no-replace
  materialization behavior across the two selections.

Neither durability mode syncs directories or promises crash recovery or
power-loss durability. `Verdict`, default evidence encodings, CLI exit classes,
published profile and policy identities, and worker protocol remain compatible.
The inventory is not a freeze; wider stable claims still need their own evidence.

## Reproducible assurance bookkeeping

Bounded read-only GitHub collection now records exact source domains, original
attempts, required jobs, distinct commits, and observation times. Deterministic
offline replay produces a separate ledger proposal; it cannot overwrite inputs
or grant promotion from a saved report. Forty-seven behavioral tests cover
failure resets, reruns, duplicate commits, source binding, incomplete history,
resource bounds, and stale or forged summaries.

The preserved September 13 observation has eight qualifying category/run
entries. Every category remains ineligible and unpromoted under the unchanged
ten-distinct-commit rule. Mutation and coverage remain discovery-only.

## What the downstream measurement changes

A controlled local experiment used released Alpha.15, eight Deepr members
totaling 363,559 bytes, and two read passes per case. All 14 cases and 224 reads
passed with exact semantic, evidence, and returned-byte parity. After a discarded
warmup pair, six measured pairs alternated strategy order. Median totals for
16 calls were 3.685663 seconds without retention and 0.000056 seconds with the
selected members retained. Separate digest checks and initial admission are
excluded from those read totals.

The [complete observation](https://github.com/blisspixel/sealr-validation/tree/main/experiments/repeated-reads/observed-linux-wsl-2026-09-13)
is a local warm-cache comparison, not a full-installation or cold-cache benchmark
and not an Alpha.16 speedup claim. Retention bypasses several costs together.
No retention ceiling, source-binding check, worker pool, or runtime dependency
changed because of this experiment.

Next, measure source hashing, plan validation, worker setup, and payload
verification separately before selecting a performance change. Authenticated
abandoned-stage recovery and stronger lifecycle guarantees remain explicit
work. Dependency updates must coordinate root, fuzz, proof, and consumer
closures rather than merge incomplete lockfile changes. The [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md)
and [near-term plan](../near-term.md) give the acceptance criteria and rationale.
